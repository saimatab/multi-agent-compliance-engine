🚀 From 48 hours to 2.5 seconds: Automating EU AI Act & Procurement Guardrails.
I engineered the Multi-Agent AI Vendor Compliance Engine, a production-grade automation orchestrator that eliminates manual delays, legal exposure, and risk blind spots for high-growth fintech firms auditing complex vendor contracts under strict regulatory frameworks.

🏗️ The Core Architecture

• Master Orchestrator Pattern: Coordinates specialized multi-agent sub-routines to dynamically isolate contract metadata and flag compliance liabilities based on semantic intent rather than fragile if/else code.
• Creator-Verifier Dynamics: Runs isolated dual-LLM pipelines via Groq where an independent auditing instance cross-checks extraction outputs to entirely mitigate hallucinations.
• Multi-Mode Runtime Guardrails: Enforces Coercive (Blocking) safety gates for fatal legal failures alongside Normative (Warning) telemetry loops mapped directly to live downstream systems.
• Asynchronous Data Routing: Leverages custom JavaScript layers to ingest raw payloads, parse text chunks, and asynchronously process high-volume legal documentation.

🛠️ The Enterprise Production Stack

• Workflow Automation & Logic: n8n (Serving as the event-driven system backbone)
• AI Orchestration & RAG: LangChain + Pinecone (Regulatory data vector mapping)
• Observability & Health: LangSmith (Deep agent tracing, latency tuning, and confidence threshold tracking)
• Storage & Business Intelligence: PostgreSQL/Supabase (Transactional ledger) + Power BI Studio (Live risk exposure heatmaps)

📊 The Technical & Business Impact

• 99.9% Latency Reduction: Slashed legal triage and contract compliance assessment times from 48 hours down to 2.5 seconds per document.
• Deterministic Lineage: Completely eliminates the "black box" problem—every automated risk score points directly back to its exact regulation citation and vector chunk origin.
• Proactive Risk Mitigation: Empowers procurement teams with real-time risk visibility and automated Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) escalations prior to final sign-off.
